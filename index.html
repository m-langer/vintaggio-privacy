Vintaggio Companion App - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Vintaggio is an iOS companion app for guided motorcycle tours. It shows tour participants their itinerary, daily stages, routes, and points of interest. This privacy policy explains how Vintaggio uses the personal data we collect from you when you use the app.

What data do we collect?

Vintaggio collects the following data:

  • Personal identification information: your name, email address, an optional profile picture, and a unique user ID created when you register.
  • Tour participation data: which tours you are a member of, your role in them (owner, guide, or rider), and when you joined.
  • Issue reports: the message you write when you report a problem, together with the app version, your device model (for example "iPhone16,2"), and your iOS version.
  • Your language preference, so the app and server can respond in your language.
  • Technical server logs, including your IP address, generated automatically when the app communicates with our servers.

Vintaggio does not collect your device location. It does not contain advertising or analytics software and does not track you across other apps or websites.

How do we collect your data?

You directly provide Vintaggio with most of the data we collect. We collect data and process data when you:

  • Register for an account or sign in.
  • Use the app to view your tours, stages, and points of interest.
  • Voluntarily send us feedback through the "Report an Issue" feature.

Vintaggio may also receive your data indirectly from the following source:

  • The tour operator who organizes your tour, when they add you as a member of that tour.

How will we use your data?

Vintaggio collects your data so that we can:

  • Create and manage your account and sign you in securely.
  • Show you the itinerary, stages, and route information of the tours you take part in.
  • Let your tour guide see who is part of their group.
  • Understand and fix problems you report to us.
  • Keep the service secure and working reliably.

We do not share your data with any partner companies, and we do not sell your data. Your data is only visible to the members and guides of the tours you belong to, to the extent needed to run the tour.

How do we store your data?

Vintaggio securely stores your data on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the EU region eu-west-1 (Ireland). Sign-in is handled by Amazon Cognito. All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).

On your device, the app stores your sign-in tokens in the iOS Keychain and keeps a local cache of tour data and downloaded route files so the app works offline. This local data is removed when you sign out or delete the app.

Vintaggio will keep your account and tour data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Me → Delete Account; this removes your account and associated personal data from our systems. If you own a tour that still has other members, you will be asked to hand over or remove that tour first. Issue reports are kept until the reported problem is resolved and are then deleted. Server logs are deleted on a rolling basis.

Marketing

Vintaggio does not send marketing communications and does not pass your data to any other organization for marketing purposes. We will only email you in direct response to a request or report you have made.

What are your data protection rights?

Vintaggio would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access – You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification – You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate, or complete information you believe is incomplete.
  • The right to erasure – You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions. You can also do this yourself at any time via Me → Delete Account in the app.
  • The right to restrict processing – You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to object to processing – You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to data portability – You have the right to request that we transfer the data we have collected to another organization, or directly to you, under certain conditions.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address in How to contact us.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to collect standard log information and remember settings between visits. The Vintaggio app itself does not use cookies. However, when you sign in, the app opens a secure sign-in page operated by Amazon Cognito, and that page uses cookies. For further general information, visit allaboutcookies.org.

How do we use cookies?

Cookies are used only during sign-in, to:

  • Keep your sign-in session active so you do not have to re-enter your password every time.
  • Protect the sign-in process against forgery and abuse.

What types of cookies do we use?

  • Functionality – strictly necessary session cookies set by Amazon Cognito on the sign-in page. There are no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking cookies.

How to manage cookies

The sign-in page opens in an isolated system web view managed by iOS. Its cookies are removed when you sign out of the app or delete the app. You cannot browse the web with it, and its cookies are not shared with Safari or other apps.

Privacy policies of other apps and websites

When you start navigation for a route, Vintaggio hands the route over to Apple Maps or Google Maps on your device. Our privacy policy applies only to Vintaggio, so once another app or website opens, you should read its own privacy policy.
