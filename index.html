Privacy Policy — Vintaggio iOS App

Effective date: 9 September 2026

Vintaggio is a companion app for guided motorcycle tours. It shows participants their tour itinerary, daily stages, routes, and points of interest. This policy explains what personal data the app processes, why, and what your rights are.

1. Who is responsible

The controller for data processed through this app is:

Moritz Langer
Email: moritz1.langer@gmail.com

Contact this address for any privacy question or request.

2. What data we process

DataWhere it comes fromWhy

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking of your behaviour across apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of your data for others' purposes.
  • No access to your device location. When you start navigation, the app hands the route over to Apple Maps or Google Maps; from that point, that app's own privacy policy applies.

4. Legal basis

We process account and tour data because it is necessary to provide the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Issue reports and technical logs are processed based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Where your data lives

All server-side data is stored and processed on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the EU region eu-west-1 (Ireland). Sign-in is handled by Amazon Cognito. AWS acts as our processor under a data processing agreement; data is not transferred outside the EU as part of normal operation.

On your device, the app stores your sign-in tokens in the iOS Keychain and keeps a local cache of tour data and downloaded route files so the app works offline. This local data is removed when you sign out or delete the app.

6. How long we keep data

Account and tour data are kept for as long as your account exists. Issue reports are kept until the issue is resolved and then deleted. Server logs are deleted on a rolling basis.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account directly in the app under Me → Delete Account. This removes your account and associated personal data from our systems. If you own a tour that still has other members, you will be asked to hand over or remove that tour first. You can also request deletion by email at the address above.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, contact the address in section 1. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Children

The app is intended for adult participants of guided motorcycle tours and is not directed at children.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
